01 / Research

Security signals, calibrated.

VULNERA ranks risky C/C++ functions for analyst review across a forward temporal test split.

The experiment combines PrimeVul, DiverseVul, Big-Vul, CVEfixes, and SecVulEval, training on older functions and evaluating on later vulnerability data.

The deployed detector prioritizes recall and presents contributing windows, signature evidence, attribution, and optional local explanations instead of claiming autonomous vulnerability detection.
