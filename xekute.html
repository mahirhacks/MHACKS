01 / Product

Every assessment artifact, connected.

Xekute is an in-development workbench for keeping authorized security testing connected, inspectable, and bounded.

Define scope, authorization, testing windows, rate limits, restricted techniques, and granular authority before active work begins.

Captured traffic, tool output, hypotheses, evidence, findings, standards coverage, and reports stay linked to their provenance in a local project workspace.

Scope first. Evidence throughout.
