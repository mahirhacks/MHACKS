01 / Product

Teach with a model, not another paragraph.

Mentora turns a learner’s question into a short, visual, voice-performed teaching turn.

GPT-5.6 chooses the teaching strategy and visual metaphor while a strict local validator checks size, references, supported operations, and script safety before anything changes.

A prepared-turn executor runs tools against a cloned board, stores the verified snapshot, and gives the realtime performer only the validated teaching line and authoritative board context.
