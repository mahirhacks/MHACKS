01 / Product

An application system that stays yours.

Joblicator turns a scattered job search into one deliberate, reviewable workflow.

Maintain a structured profile, capture postings, select a CV template per role, and track each application from intake to final export.

A configurable three-stage Ollama pipeline builds source-grounded, ATS-focused documents with quality checks, parser verification, and failure-tolerant batch processing.
